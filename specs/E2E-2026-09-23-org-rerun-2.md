## Defects

1. **A bought space opens every app of the suite, whatever was bought.**
   (Medium; high once real money is taken.) Steps 3 and 5. The organisation
   bought `sign.subscription` (USD 29 a month). The licence grants
   `modules []`; the bridge session records `entitlements ["sign"]`; the
   launcher offers the owner **39 apps**; and `GET /api/entitlements` answers

   (`consumer/api/platform/src/entitlement-resolver.js` lines 31 to 42), the
   stub grants every key when `RUTBA_ENTITLEMENTS` is unset

   else narrows it.

   (`workers/provisioning/src/steps.js`, the `register` step from line 100, `domains: ctx.claim.domains ?? []` at line 111);
