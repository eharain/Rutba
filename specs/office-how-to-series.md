# Rutba Office: how-to films for every app, and a Data Files series

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-23: "we need to do detailed videos on how to
about each app functions and detailed series on data files."

These are teaching films, not the marketing films of 2026-10-21 (one per
app, problem then strongest feature). Each one shows one task from start
to finish, at the pace of someone doing it for the first time.

## How every episode is made

- **One task per episode,** 3 to 6 minutes, named by the task ("Track
  changes and accept them", not "Review tab").
- **Shot on the released version,** on sample files and invented people
  only, as the other films are. No real account, address or screen appears.
- **Narrated** with the same voice as the app films (bm_fable), with .srt
  captions, chapters in the description, and the sample file the episode
  uses downloadable from office.rutba.io where there is one.
- **One playlist per app** on @RutbaOffice, plus the Data Files series as
  its own playlist in order. Each episode links to its app's section of
  office.rutba.io/apps; Data Files episodes also link to the sample they use.
- **When:** after the current round of Data Files sample films, and only
  once the known issues are fixed and released ("we do films when we fix
  the known issues"). A finished episode is not reshot for a minor change.
- **Who:** the video session shoots and builds; LinkedIn and Youtube update
  uploads; the website sessions link them; the Office session checks each
  script against the app before shooting and is told if a film shows
  anything wrong.

## The Data Files series (in order, about 24 episodes)

A business's database built from nothing, then the samples used to show
what a finished one does. Larkspur Corner Store (a shop) runs through the
first part, so each episode builds on the one before.

**Part 1: a database of your own**
1. What Data Files is: tables, records and one file you keep
2. Start from a ready-made database, or an empty one
3. Make a table and design its fields: text, numbers, currency, dates, yes/no
4. Required, unique and a default: rules a field keeps
5. Enter records in the datasheet; sort, filter and find
6. Import a CSV or a spreadsheet as a new table
7. Relationships: a sale and its lines, a customer and their sales

**Part 2: asking questions**
8. Your first query: pick the fields and the criteria
9. Totals: counts, sums and averages, grouped, and rounded
10. Joining tables in a query
11. The same question in SQL

**Part 3: forms people use**
12. A form from the wizard, and laying it out
13. Lists that pick a record by name, and searching a long list
14. Subforms: a sale with its lines on one screen

**Part 4: paper and other apps**
15. Reports grouped with totals, printed or saved as PDF
16. Print templates: an invoice, a receipt, a letter
17. Mail merge: letters from a table in Documents

**Part 5: an office of people**
18. Protect a database and add people with their own passwords
19. Roles: what each person sees and may change
20. See as a role, and the trail of who changed what

**Part 6: one database on many computers**
21. Link a table to PostgreSQL or MySQL
22. Live records: what every computer linked to the engine sees
23. Working with linked tables in queries, forms and reports

**Part 7: the samples as a tour** (one short episode per group of samples,
showing a finished database at work: shops, services, property, people)
24. A tour of the ready-made businesses

Synced copies and the other sources (2.18.0) add episodes once released.

## The other twelve apps (about 70 episodes)

**Documents**
1. Open a Word file and save it back as Word
2. Styles, headings and a table of contents
3. Lists, tables and pictures in the text
4. Page setup, headers, footers and page numbers
5. Track changes and comments, then accept or reject them
6. Templates: start from one, keep your own
7. Markdown: edit a README and give it back unchanged

**Worksheets**
1. Open an Excel workbook; formulas that recalculate
2. Formatting numbers, dates and conditional colours
3. Formulas from SUM to lookups, with the range drawn as you drag
4. Sort, filter and tables
5. Charts from a range
6. PivotTables
7. Data from Picture: a printed table into rows
8. Old files: Lotus, dBASE, Access and .xls into .xlsx

**Presentations**
1. Open a PowerPoint deck; layouts and themes
2. Pictures, shapes and moving them by hand
3. Design Ideas from a slide's own words
4. Speaker notes and the presenter view on a second screen
5. Recording narration and a camera cameo

**PDF**
1. Read, find and copy text
2. Reorder, rotate, delete, insert, split and merge pages
3. Fill in a form and sign it
4. Redact names and figures, applied to a new copy
5. Recognise Text: make a scan searchable

**Mail**
1. Add an account: an address and a password, or sign in to Gmail and Outlook
2. Many accounts at once from a pasted list
3. One inbox, folders, rules and junk
4. Open an old Outlook .pst or a Mac .olm and search it with today's mail
5. Trackers named, and the way off a list
6. Out of office and signatures

**Calendar**
1. Month, week, day and agenda
2. A meeting with an invitation by email
3. Repeats and time zones
4. Answering an invitation from Mail
5. Sync with a CalDAV account; Hijri dates beside the days

**Contacts**
1. Bring in an address book from a .vcf or a .csv
2. Keep a sender from Mail in one click
3. Sync with a CardDAV account

**Pictures**
1. A folder of thousands, open at once
2. Ratings, tags and searching folders
3. Resize, convert and rename a whole folder
4. Copy Text from Picture

**Image**
1. Crop, straighten and correct: levels, curves, white balance
2. Selections, brushes, clone and heal
3. Layers, masks and blend modes, saved as a project
4. Edit in Image from the other apps, the original untouched

**Video**
1. The player: speed, repeat, captions, picture in picture
2. A first edit: clips on the timeline, split and trim
3. Titles, captions, transitions and sound
4. Speed, Freeze Frame and Reverse
5. An introduction scene by scene with the teleprompter
6. Export to MP4 or WebM for where it is going

**Recorder**
1. Record the screen with the camera in a corner
2. Scenes to switch between while recording
3. A region, a title and a logo
4. Pause, the stop key, and editing the recording in Video
5. Go Live to YouTube, Facebook or Twitch

**Talk**
1. Sign in and add a contact (both of you say yes)
2. Messages and calls, and reading out the safety code
3. When two computers cannot reach each other: a relay

**The suite itself** (its own short playlist)
1. Download, install and the first start: choosing apps
2. Languages: English, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi
3. Updates, and where your files stay
